# The ground-block failure — why a full ceremony can still return nothing

Read this when composing a `## Ground` block, or when a run's independence is
questioned. It is the case behind § *The `## Ground` block carries relevant
context only* in `SKILL.md`, kept out of the body so the body stays loadable at
invocation size.

## What happened

A ceremony spawned four seats over work its own composer had authored.

The composer pre-registered its expected findings, held them out of the task
text, and then handed one seat the exact probe by which it had already obtained
one of those findings. The seat duly found it. The audit seat ruled the run's
independence **unestablishable** — the composer, in its words, "had already run
the probe, saw the result, wrote it down as a prediction, then handed the probe
to the seat."

Two further defects surfaced in the same audit:

- Three seats carried composer-stated facts.
- The composer's own disclosure said "three seats" while naming two.

## Why this is the load-bearing case

**Every artifact this skill asks for was present.** Pre-registration existed and
was held separate. The players were isolated sub-agents with clean contexts. An
audit seat was composed and it ran. Nothing in the ceremony's checklist was
skipped.

That is precisely why none of it discharged the duty. The apparatus is not the
mechanism; the ground blocks are. A run can satisfy every structural requirement
and still be a mirror, if what reached the players told them what looking would
turn up.

**Handing over the method hands over the finding with one step of deniability
attached.** A probe is not neutral context merely because it is phrased as an
instruction rather than a conclusion. The composer never wrote down the answer —
it wrote down the route, which in a capable model is the same thing arriving one
inference later.

## The repair, and that it worked

The re-run sent seven reviewers whose ground blocks carried **the facts** and
stated in as many words that no prediction was offered and none was wanted.

They returned findings the composer had not anticipated, several of them against
the composer's own work. That last clause is the evidence the repair was real: a
seat that can only return what the composer expected is decoration, and these
seats came back with things the composer did not want.

## The one question

Applied to every line before it enters a ground block:

> Does this let the player look, or does it tell the player what looking will
> turn up?

A line that survives that question is ground. A line that does not is an
expectation, and an expectation has one destination and it is not a prompt — it
goes to the collaborator, in the chat channel, as a question.

## The corollary for handoffs

A handoff document that carries a prior session's read of what is wrong with an
artifact is useful to a *builder* and disqualifying for any *evaluator seat*.
When a session both builds from such a document and validates the result, the
document, its analysis, and any restatement of it stay out of every player
prompt — including the probe by which the analysis was obtained.
